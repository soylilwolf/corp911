Suspension & Reinstatement Resources

What Is a Corporate Suspension?

A suspension occurs when a state agency (typically the Franchise Tax Board or Secretary of State) revokes a corporation's or LLC's right to conduct business, usually due to unpaid taxes or unfiled statements.

Learn more →

Common Causes

Failure to file annual reports, unpaid franchise taxes, not maintaining a registered agent, or failure to file a Statement of Information are the most frequent causes.

Learn more →

Reinstatement Process

Reinstatement typically involves filing overdue documents, paying back taxes and penalties, and submitting a reinstatement application to the appropriate state agency.

Learn more →

Why Act Quickly?

A suspended corporation cannot legally enter contracts, file lawsuits, or defend itself in court. Officers may also face personal liability for business debts incurred during suspension.
